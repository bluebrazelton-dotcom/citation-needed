# 🎓 Citation Needed: The Academic Career Simulator

**[▶️ Play Live in Your Browser: bluebrazelton.com/citation-needed/](https://bluebrazelton.com/citation-needed/)**

**Citation Needed** is a single-file, browser-based pixel-art incremental and active management game that simulates the full arc of an academic career—from an over-caffeinated Undergraduate Assistant all the way to an Endowed Chair.

Built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 `<canvas>`, and Tailwind CSS, the game captures both the joyful absurdity and the grind of scholarly life across four distinct academic disciplines.

---

## 🏛️ Four Playable Disciplines (With Distinct Mechanics)

Switch paths at any time—each discipline maintains its own save state, custom pixel-art workspace, visible equipment props, and signature passive mechanics:

* **🧬 Bench Scientist (STEM):** Manage a wet lab floor, time PCR/pipette sweet-spots, and scale high-capital instrumentation from Vortex Mixers up to a Cryo-EM Structural Suite.
  * *Signature Mechanic — Lab Automation Synergy:* Every hired researcher boosts equipment output by **+5%**, paired with higher R01 grant ceilings.
* **📋 Social Scientist:** Run a qualitative and mixed-methods studio, code emergent themes, and balance IRB amendments with foundation proposals.
  * *Signature Mechanic — Mixed-Methods Triangulation:* Hitting sweet-spot timing combos generates immediate bonus Foundation Grant funding (**+35%**) alongside **+15%** bonus peer-review citations.
* **📜 Historian:** Work inside a mahogany Special Collections archive, decipher paleography, and turn archival finds into landmark university press monographs.
  * *Signature Mechanic — Archival Monograph Gravitas:* Each published monograph awards **1.5x Citations** and permanently compounds passive output by **+20%**.
* **⚖️ Legal Scholar:** Navigate Constitutional Chambers, Shepardize 300-footnote articles, and ride the expedited review wave up to a flagship Law Review.
  * *Signature Mechanic — Expedited Law Review Preemption:* Articles draft **28% faster** (`+32%` per step) and award **+35% bonus Citations** plus immediate Clinic Stipends upon acceptance.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Features

* **Active Sweet-Spot Timing Bar:** Time your clicks inside the green and gold target zones to build up to an **8x Combo** multiplier—no passive waiting walls.
* **Gatekeeper & Reviewer #2 Showdowns:** Submitting a manuscript triggers a *Revise & Resubmit* decision letter. Choose your diplomatic, empirical, or procedural rebuttal strategy—every path overcomes the gatekeeper with unique resource rewards.
* **Clickable Room Anomalies:** Watch the workspace for unbalanced centrifuges, surprise IRB amendments, misfiled diplomatic trunks, and free department seminar food. Click them for instant buffs (or hire a Tier 4 Manager/Archivist to auto-resolve them).
* **Visible Progression:** Every piece of equipment you buy appears on your room's back shelves, every student or postdoc you hire wanders the floor with speech bubbles, and your scholar's sprite upgrades regalia across all **6 Career Ranks**.
* **Automatic Save Persistence:** Progress across all four paths saves automatically to your browser's `localStorage`.
